Cookie Policy

Kiver uses cookies and similar browser storage only where needed to operate the service securely and reliably.

Essential cookies

Kiver's account system uses an essential, HTTP-only session cookie named kiver_session. It keeps you signed in and supports authenticated actions. It is Secure, SameSite and path-scoped to the site. This cookie is required for account functionality.

Payment checkout

Paid promotion checkout is provided by Paddle. Paddle may use cookies or similar technologies as part of its secure checkout, fraud prevention, payment processing and related services. Those technologies are governed by Paddle's own privacy and cookie information in addition to this policy.

No advertising cookies

Kiver does not currently use advertising cookies or behavioral advertising trackers. If that changes, this policy and the site's consent controls will be updated before the relevant non-essential technology is used.

Managing cookies

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Removing the Kiver session cookie will sign you out and may prevent account features from working until you sign in again.
