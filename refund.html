Refund Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

We want you to feel the Complete Report was worth it. Here's exactly how refunds work.

1. Complete Report (one-time purchase)

Because the Complete Report is a digital product delivered instantly, refund requests are handled case by case. You're eligible for a full refund if:

  • The report failed to generate or was not delivered due to a technical error on our end, or
  • The report is clearly, substantially inaccurate or incomplete relative to what was promised on the pricing page.

To request a refund, email support@getkiver.com within 14 days of purchase with your order details and the reason for the request. We'll respond within 5 business days.

2. Monitor and Agency subscriptions

You can cancel a subscription at any time. Cancelling stops future renewals, but we do not provide partial refunds for the remaining days in an already-paid billing period. If you're charged due to a billing error (for example, a duplicate charge), contact us and we'll correct it.

3. How refunds are processed

All payments are processed by Paddle.com Market Limited, our merchant of record. Approved refunds are issued back to your original payment method through Paddle and may take several business days to appear, depending on your bank or card provider.

4. Contact

For any billing question, email support@getkiver.com.
