How to Make Money on Telegram: 7 Realistic Ways

Published October 4, 2026 · By the Kiver team

On this page

  2. Which method fits you?
  3. A simple roadmap from zero to first income
  4. Scams to avoid

  6. Frequently asked questions

Telegram has roughly a billion monthly users, built-in payments, channels with no follower cap and a bot platform anyone can use for free. That makes it one of the few places where a solo creator can build a small business without paying a platform to reach their own audience. It also makes it a magnet for get-rich-quick scams. This guide covers the methods that actually exist, what each one requires, and how to spot the ones that never pay.

Key takeaways
  • Telegram shares 50% of ad revenue with channel owners, but you need a real, engaged audience first.
  • The most dependable income comes from selling something: a paid community, a bot, a service or a digital product.
  • Anything that asks you to deposit money to "unlock" earnings is a scam. Real methods never work that way.
  • Pick one method, test it with a small audience, then add a second.

7 ways to make money on Telegram

1. Earn a share of ad revenue on your channel

Telegram's official program gives channel owners 50% of the revenue from ads displayed in their channels. You can see your numbers in Channel Settings, then Statistics, then Monetization, and withdraw through Fragment. Because Telegram ads are matched to the channel rather than to personal data, your topic is your targeting. A tightly focused channel (crypto tools, language learning, local jobs) is worth more to advertisers than a general one. Eligibility rules and minimum audience sizes change, so check Telegram's current requirements before you plan around this.

2. Run a paid channel or community

Charge for access to a private channel or group: premium research, a job board, a language-learning club, a niche market newsletter, coaching with a peer group. You can collect payment with Telegram Stars subscriptions or an external payment provider connected through a bot. The rule that decides whether this works is recurring value. People pay monthly for something new every week, not for an archive. A common pattern is a free public channel that proves your quality and a paid tier for the deeper material. If your niche touches financial advice or investment signals, check the regulations in your country first.

3. Build a bot or mini app people pay for

Bots are software that lives inside Telegram, and software can charge. Common models are a free tier plus a paid plan, pay-per-use credits, or one-time purchases. Digital goods sold inside bots and mini apps are generally expected to use Telegram Stars, so read the current Bot Payments documentation before you design your pricing. A bot needs far less audience than a channel because discovery happens through directories and search. If you have never built one, start with our guide on how to create a Telegram bot.

4. Sell your skills as a freelancer

Businesses and creators keep asking for the same things: custom bots, mini apps, channel setup, community moderation, copywriting and growth. You can find clients in Telegram job channels, freelance marketplaces and by direct outreach. Your best portfolio is your own work, so build two or three small bots and list them on Kiver where a client can see them live.

5. Use affiliate links and referral programs

Recommend tools you actually use, such as hosting, design tools or apps, and earn a commission when someone signs up. It works best inside a channel that already has trust. Always disclose affiliate links, keep them relevant to the post, and avoid dropping links in other people's groups: most communities ban that quickly.

6. Sell sponsored posts directly

Once your channel has a consistent niche audience, brands in that niche will pay to be featured. Price by average views per post and audience quality, not just subscriber count, and keep sponsored content clearly labelled and sparse so the channel stays worth reading.

7. Sell digital products

Templates, guides, presets, spreadsheets, short courses and tools are cheap to produce once and can be delivered automatically by a bot after payment. Your channel is the shop window, the bot is the checkout and delivery.

Which method fits you?

The table below rates each method by how big an audience you need, how much ongoing work it takes and what kind of income it produces. If you are starting from zero, the methods with a low audience requirement (bots, freelancing) are usually the fastest way to a first payment.

A simple roadmap from zero to first income

  1. Pick one niche. One topic and one audience beats a general channel every time.
  2. Give value for free. Start a channel, a bot or a stream of useful posts so people have a reason to follow you.
  3. Find your first 100 people. Use relevant communities, directories like the Kiver directory, and trackable deep links.
  4. Add one offer. Ads, a paid tier or a product. One, not five.
  5. Measure and repeat. Keep what converts, drop what does not.

Scams to avoid

Because Telegram is open and fast, it is full of fake money-making schemes. Walk away from any of these:

  • Anything that asks you to deposit money before you can "unlock" or "withdraw" earnings.
  • Guaranteed daily returns, "risk-free" trading signals or bots that promise profit.
  • "Task" or "like and subscribe" jobs that pay a little, then demand a fee to pay out more.
  • Referral pyramids where income depends on recruiting others.
  • Strangers who message you a job offer, or any bot that asks for your seed phrase or login code.

If something looks like this, use Telegram's Report option and move on. A real method makes you money by serving customers or an audience, not by taking your deposit.

Frequently asked questions

How much money can you make on Telegram?

There is no honest single number. Ad revenue scales with channel views, paid communities with price multiplied by members and retention, and freelancing with your rates and client load. Anyone quoting a typical income without showing the math is usually selling something.

Do I need a big audience to make money on Telegram?

Not for every method. Building bots, freelancing and selling digital products can work with a small audience. Ad revenue sharing and sponsored posts need a larger, engaged one.

Can you make money with a Telegram bot?

Yes. Common models are subscriptions, pay-per-use credits and premium tiers, plus building bots for clients as a freelancer. Digital goods sold in bots generally use Telegram Stars.

Is it free to start?

Creating a channel or a bot costs nothing. Hosting a bot ranges from free tiers to a few dollars a month, depending on traffic.

Do I have to pay tax on Telegram income?

In most countries, yes. Rules vary by location, so this is not tax advice. Check with a local accountant or your tax authority.
