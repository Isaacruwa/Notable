Most people picture trading as a screen full of charts, a trading account and a lot of stress. Mine is a chat window. I send a chart screenshot to an AI bot on Telegram, and it reads the structures on the chart and sends back a signal. That simple routine brings in roughly $250 to $500 a month for me, and in this guide I will show you how it works and how you can set up something similar.

Key takeaways
  • A screenshot signal bot needs no API keys, no passwords and no access to your exchange account.
  • You send a chart, the bot spots specific structures, and you get a signal in seconds.
  • You make every trade decision yourself.
  • I earn roughly $250 to $500 a month with this setup: a steady side income, not overnight riches.

What a screenshot analysis bot does

The idea is simple. You take a screenshot of a chart, send it to the bot on Telegram, and the bot analyzes it. It looks for particular structures it has been built to recognize, then replies with a signal.

  1. Send a clear screenshot of the chart you are watching.
  2. Wait a few seconds while the bot analyzes the structures on it.
  3. Get your signal in the same chat, ready to use.

Why I like this approach

It is private and safe. The bot never connects to your exchange or broker. It only sees the screenshot you send, so there are no keys to create and no passwords to share.

It is fast. Instead of staring at charts, I get a read in seconds, wherever I am. Telegram works on any phone, so there is nothing to install.

It is consistent. A bot looks for the same structures the same way every time. That takes some of the guesswork and emotion out of the decision.

You stay in control. The bot gives signals. You choose whether to act on one, how much to put in, and when to stop.

A simple routine you can follow

  • Use clear screenshots. Include the full chart, and keep the timeframe the same so your signals are comparable.
  • Check each signal against your own view. The bot is a second pair of eyes, not a replacement for your judgment.
  • Start with small trade sizes while you learn how the signals behave.
  • Keep a log. Write down each signal, what you did and what happened. After a month you will know exactly what works for you.
  • Grow slowly. Add size only once your log shows results you are happy with.

What $250 to $500 a month really means

Your own results will depend on how much you trade with, how disciplined you are and how the market behaves, so use my range as proof the approach can work, not as a promise.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to give the bot my exchange login or API keys?

No. A screenshot bot only sees the image you send, so there are no keys or passwords involved.

Do I need trading experience?

It helps, but you do not need to be an expert. The bot does the first read of the chart, and you build your own judgment as you keep a log.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. Everything happens inside Telegram, so it works on any phone or computer where you use the app.

How much can I earn?

I make roughly $250 to $500 a month. Your results will depend on your capital, discipline and the market, so treat my range as an example and not a guarantee.
