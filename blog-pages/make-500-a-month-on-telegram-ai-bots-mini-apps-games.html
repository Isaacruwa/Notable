How to Make $500 a Month on Telegram With AI Bots, Mini Apps and Games

Five hundred dollars a month is a realistic first income goal on Telegram. You do not need a huge audience or a big budget. You need one useful bot or Mini App, a clear way to charge for it, and enough people who find it. This guide shows the math, four lanes that work (AI bots, Mini Apps, games and social or dating bots), real verified bots to study, and a 30-day plan to get started.

Key takeaways
  • $500 a month is roughly 38,500 Telegram Stars at the rate Telegram pays developers, which is far fewer customers than most people expect.
  • The fastest paths are a subscription, a premium pack for an AI bot, or small in-app purchases in a Mini App game.
  • Pick a lane where people already pay: AI tools, games, entertainment or social features.
  • Being easy to find matters as much as the bot. Listing on Kiver puts your bot in front of people who are browsing for exactly this.

What $500 a month actually takes

Telegram's developer terms currently pay about $0.013 for every Star you receive, so $500 a month is around 38,500 Stars. That number can change, so check the current terms before you price anything, and see our full guide to making money with Telegram Stars for payouts and fees. The same goal looks very different depending on what you sell.

Look at the first row. About 154 people paying 250 Stars a month gets you there. That is a small community, not a mass audience. Treat these numbers as a planning tool, not a promise: many bots earn nothing, and the ones that earn usually solve one problem very well.

Lane 1: AI bots

People already like asking an AI for help inside a chat app they open all day. The classic model is simple: give new users a few free tries, then sell credit packs or a monthly plan. Good ideas are narrow ones, such as an AI that writes product descriptions, turns voice notes into text, edits photos or analyzes a chart screenshot.

  • How it earns: credit packs, a monthly subscription, or a premium tier with faster or better results.
  • Watch out for: every reply costs you money in AI usage, so price each pack above your cost, and put a limit on free tries.

Lane 2: Mini Apps

A Mini App opens as a full screen inside Telegram, so it can be a shop, a planner, a booking page or a tool with a proper interface. They suit anything that needs more than buttons and text. Our beginner's guide to Telegram Mini Apps explains how they work and how to launch one.

  • How it earns: a premium tier, one-off unlocks, or a monthly pass paid in Stars.
  • Watch out for: keep the first version tiny. One screen that does one job beats a large app nobody finishes.

Lane 3: Games

Quizzes, word games, puzzles, trivia and casual arcade games work well in a chat, because friends can share them in one tap. Browse the games on Kiver to see what is out there.

  • How it earns: extra attempts, hints, cosmetic items or an ad-free pass, each at a small price. Small prices add up when many people play.
  • Watch out for: make the free version genuinely fun. Avoid promising rewards or real-money prizes you cannot legally or reliably deliver.

Lane 4: Social and dating bots

Bots that help people meet, chat or find a language partner are popular because they solve a very human need. Think icebreaker games, interest-based matching, anonymous chat rooms or friendship finders.

  • How it earns: premium filters, profile boosts or a monthly plan that gives better matches.
  • Watch out for: safety is the product. Ask users to confirm they are adults, add report and block buttons, moderate actively and keep content within Telegram's terms. People will only pay for a place they trust.

A 30-day plan

  1. Week 1: choose one problem. Pick a lane and write one sentence: "This bot helps [who] do [what] in [how long]." If you cannot say it in one sentence, narrow it.
  2. Week 2: build the smallest version. Follow our guide to creating a Telegram bot and ship one feature that works.
  3. Week 3: add a way to pay. Offer a free trial and a paid option in Stars. Keep it to one or two prices.
  4. Week 4: get found. List your bot on Kiver, then share it where your audience already is. Our guide on getting more users for your Telegram bot covers what works.

After the first month, look at two numbers: how many people try the bot, and how many of them pay. Improve whichever is weaker before you add new features.

Frequently asked questions

Can you really make $500 a month on Telegram?

It is a realistic goal for a focused bot or Mini App with a clear way to charge, but it is not guaranteed. Many bots earn little or nothing, and results depend on your idea, your audience and how easy you are to find.

How many users do I need to earn $500 a month?

It depends on your price. At about $0.013 per Star, 154 subscribers paying 250 Stars a month, or 77 buyers of a 500 Star pack, would reach about $500. Plan with your own prices and the current Telegram terms.

Do I need to know how to code?

Coding helps and gives you the most control. No-code bot builders exist for simple bots, and you can start with a basic version and improve it as you learn.

Are dating and social bots allowed on Telegram?

Social and matching bots are common, but you must follow Telegram's terms. Keep content age-appropriate, ask users to confirm they are adults, and give people tools to report and block.

How do I get paid?

Payments for digital goods in bots and Mini Apps are made in Telegram Stars. To cash out you need at least 1,000 Stars that are 21 days old, and you withdraw through Fragment to a TON wallet. Our Stars guide explains the steps.
