Privacy

Kiver stores account credentials, bot listing data and community contributions required to operate the directory. Passwords are salted and hashed and sessions use secure HTTP-only cookies.

Public listings

Bot names, public Telegram metadata, categories and community contributions may be displayed publicly because Kiver is a discovery directory.

Payments

When you purchase a Kiver promotional service, Paddle.com acts as the Merchant of Record and processes payment, billing, tax and transaction information. Kiver receives the information needed to provide and manage the purchased service, such as transaction and subscription identifiers and account contact details. Kiver does not store full payment card details.

Telegram metadata

Submission fetches publicly exposed metadata from the supplied Telegram bot page. Kiver does not request private Telegram messages.
