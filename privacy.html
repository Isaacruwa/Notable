Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Kiver (getkiver.com) collects, why, and how it's handled.

1. What we collect

  • Scan queries. When you run a free scan, the name, brand, or company you enter is sent to our search and AI-analysis providers to generate your result. We do not require you to submit your own personal information to run a scan on someone else's name — but if you scan your own name, that query is, by nature, personal to you.
  • Payment information. If you purchase a paid product, payment details are collected and processed directly by Paddle, our payment provider — Kiver does not receive or store your card details.
  • Basic technical data. Standard hosting/analytics data such as approximate location, device type, and browser, collected automatically by our hosting provider (Vercel) for security and performance purposes.

2. Third parties involved in producing a scan

To generate a Notability Score, the query you submit is sent to:

  • Serper.dev — to search the web and news for relevant public mentions.
  • Google (Gemini API) — to analyze that search evidence and produce the score and written report.

These providers process the query only to return a result to Kiver; they are not used by Kiver to build a profile of you as a visitor.

3. How we use information

  • To run the scan you requested and show you a result.
  • To process payments for paid products, via Paddle.
  • To maintain and secure the website.

We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Data retention

At this time, Kiver does not maintain user accounts or store a history of past scans tied to an individual visitor. If this changes (for example, with the introduction of account-based report history), this policy will be updated accordingly before that feature launches.

5. Cookies

Kiver's core site does not currently use tracking or advertising cookies. If analytics or account features are added in the future, this section will be updated to reflect that.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or request deletion of personal data we hold about you. To make a request, contact support@getkiver.com.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@getkiver.com.

Kiver produces an independent, analytical score based on observable public signals. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or an official metric of Google, Meta, Instagram, OpenAI, Wikipedia, or any other platform.
