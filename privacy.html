Privacy

TGFoundry stores account credentials, bot listing data and community contributions required to operate the directory. Passwords are salted scrypt hashes and sessions use HTTP-only cookies.

Public listings

Bot names, public Telegram metadata, categories and community contributions may be displayed publicly because TGFoundry is a discovery directory.

Telegram metadata

Submission fetches publicly exposed metadata from the supplied Telegram bot page. It does not request private Telegram messages.
